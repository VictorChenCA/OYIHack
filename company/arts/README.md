# Arts planet

**Project:** Brand (`brand`)

What the agents produce here:
- `logo.svg`: the C&C mark (works at 16px and at 512px, on dark).
- `palette.json` + `palette.svg`: the brand palette with roles (background, accent, alert, gold) and a preview swatch sheet.
- `og.svg`: a 1200×630 Open Graph image.

C&C is a name-only nod to *Command & Conquer*: no EA logos, fonts or art. Missing brand input shows up as a **violet** blocker.
