Members and admins

Members are not billed by seat. A new member can use your UFO after they join or an admin adds
them. There is no approval queue.

Add a memberSection titled “Add a member”

An admin can ask your UFO:

Add alex@ufo.ai as a member and send the sign-in email.

Add pat@contractor.example as an admin. Do not notify them.

An admin can also use Add member on the Team page. Turn Notify off
to add a member without sending an email.

A teammate on the workspace’s email domain can sign in and join directly. A person on another
domain must be added by their exact address.

AdminsSection titled “Admins”

A workspace can have several admins. Admins can manage members and roles, connect Slack, enter
workspace credentials, handle billing, and open every agent.

Any member can ask who is in the workspace and which members are admins. An admin can ask your UFO
to change a role. A workspace must keep at least one active admin, so the last admin cannot be
demoted.

UFO accessSection titled “UFO access”

Non-admin members can use:

Agents open to the workspace.

Agents they created.

Private agents shared with them.

Share or remove access from the private agent’s own conversation.

Remove and restore accessSection titled “Remove and restore access”

An admin can ask your UFO to remove a member. Your UFO stops answering that member, and their
current work stops before the next step.

The member’s conversation history and memory remain. Add the member again to restore access.
