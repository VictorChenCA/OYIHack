Troubleshooting

Ask your UFO to check the current state before you reconnect an account or repeat work.

Your UFO does not answer in SlackSection titled “Your UFO does not answer in Slack”

Confirm that UFO is in the channel and mention @ufo once. After a mention starts a thread, replies
in that thread reach your UFO.

For a new member, check the email address on their Slack account. A person outside the workspace’s
email domain must be added before your UFO can answer.

A Slack install link failsSection titled “A Slack install link fails”

Ask your UFO for a new link. Install links are short-lived and cannot be reused. If the new link
also fails, ask your UFO to raise the problem with the team.

A connection stopped workingSection titled “A connection stopped working”

Ask your UFO to check the connection, then ask to reconnect the account. Access may have
expired or been removed in the connected service.

For GitHub, each member connects their own account. A teammate’s connection does not give another
member access to clone or push a private repository.

Your UFO cannot find a remembered factSection titled “Your UFO cannot find a remembered fact”

Ask where the fact was stated. Private memory, workspace memory, a private room, and an external
shared channel have different scopes. Restate the fact in the scope where it must be available.

A task did not postSection titled “A task did not post”

A scheduled task sends no message when it finds nothing to report. Open Tasks, then Runs,
to inspect its conversation. Check the task state, connected accounts, destination, and workspace balance.

You need to stop workSection titled “You need to stop work”

Use the stop button on the web or press Esc in the terminal. You cannot stop work from Slack.

A message is waitingSection titled “A message is waiting”

Check the workspace balance. A member message is held when there is not enough credit. It runs
automatically after an admin adds credit.

The problem remainsSection titled “The problem remains”

Ask your UFO to pass the symptom, affected account, time, and failed action to the UFO team. Do not
send a credential or secret.
